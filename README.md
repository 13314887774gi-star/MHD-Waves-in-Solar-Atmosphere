# MHD-Waves-in-Solar-Atmosphere

My dissertation broadly discussed Magnetohydrodynamics with the study of the interaction of a fluid with a magnetic field. We are interested in the behaviors of magnetic waves in the solar atmosphere where the magnetic field plays an important role.

We are using the continuum approach to study MHD from a macroscopic aspect. In magnetohydrodynamics, the fluid is a conductor of electricity, with the existence of magnetic fields, motions within the fluid are induced. We introduced Maxwell’s equations, which are the key equations of electromagnetism. Also, we have a large set of equations called MHD equations. Most of the MHD equations are non-linear and difficult to solve, we need to linearise them and obtain a set of differential equations. To solve such differential equations, we use a representation in terms of the complex exponential functions.

My dissertation’s main theme is studying the wave behaviors in magnetic slabs and magnetic flux tubes. There are two types of waves in general, longitudinal waves such as sound waves; transverse waves such as Alfv´en waves. We obtained the dispersion relations of fast-mode and slow-mode magnetoacoustic waves. With the basic properties of different types of waves, we finally start our main topic - the magnetic slabs. Our goal is to find the dispersion relations and present the results graphically. To handle the complicated dispersion relations we derive, numerical methods in Python was applied.

In general, we explore the mathematical models of the physical effects of the solar atmosphere, with a combination of fluid mechanics and electromagnetism.
